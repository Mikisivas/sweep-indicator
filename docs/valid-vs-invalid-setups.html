ICT model · sweep → CISD → order block

Valid vs. invalid setup, candle by candle

Both charts start identically: price sells off and sweeps the previous low (sell-side liquidity) inside a 4H fair value gap. What separates a trade from no-trade is one thing — whether a candle closes back through the opens of the down-close series that did the sweep (the CISD level).

1 · Sweep — stops under a prior low get taken 2 · 4H FVG — HTF zone price is trading into 3 · CISD — close through the sweep series’ opens 4 · Order block — the sweep series becomes the entry zone
Sweep candle CISD confirmation (valid) Failed / invalid candles Neutral down / up 4H FVG (BISI) Order block Protected low (stop)

Valid setup Trade

  1. Sweep (orange): the wick trades below the previous low — stops are taken — inside the 4H BISI, and price snaps back above the level.
  2. CISD (green): a candle closes above 57.0, the open of the first down-close candle in the sweep series. Delivery flips from sell-side to buy-side.
  3. Entry: the sweep series is now an order block; go long on the retest of it.
  4. Stop & target: stop below the protected low; target the opposing (buy-side) liquidity at the session high.

Valid — sweep + close above CISD level = confirmed shift. Entry on OB retest.

Invalid setup No trade

  1. Sweep (orange): identical start — the previous low is swept inside the 4H BISI.
  2. No CISD (gray): price wicks above 57.0 but never closes above it. A wick is not a change in state of delivery.
  3. Invalidated: a candle closes below the swept low — the "protected" low failed; sellers are still delivering. Price continues lower.

Invalid — a sweep without a CISD close is not a setup. No order block, no entry.

The rule in one line: HTF context (price at a 4H FVG / OB) → LTF sweep of liquidity → wait for a 15-minute close through the CISD level → only then does the sweep series become an order block → enter on its retest, stop beyond the protected low/high, target opposing liquidity. Everything before that close is just a sweep — and a sweep alone can keep going against you.
