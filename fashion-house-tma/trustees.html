THE PEOPLE

BOARD OF TRUSTEES

Four people, one thread. Tap “Read bio” on any card to meet them properly.

Board members

Adaeze “Dez” Okonkwo

FOUNDER & CREATIVE DIRECTOR

Dez started Urban Thread from a sewing machine in Surulere, printing tees for skate crews and market runners. She still signs off every drop by hand and insists on wearing samples for a month before anything ships.

Tobi Alade

CHIEF EXECUTIVE OFFICER

Tobi ran logistics for two of West Africa's biggest fashion retailers before joining the board. He keeps the drops on schedule, the pop-ups stocked, and the brand stubbornly independent.

Mariam Bello

CHIEF FINANCIAL OFFICER

A chartered accountant with a sneaker archive that outgrew her spare room, Mariam prices every run so the brand never has to compromise on fabric weight to make the numbers work.

Kelechi Eze

HEAD OF DESIGN

Kelechi sketches on danfo rides and turns street signage, road markings and market typography into the graphic language of every collection. If it's printed on a Thread piece, it started in his notebook.
